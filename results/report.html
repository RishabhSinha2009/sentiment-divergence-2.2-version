News sentiment vs. price divergence

Does a stock catch up when its news turns positive (or negative) but its price goes the other way? Tested on 30 large US stocks, 2015 to today, with FinBERT-scored headlines.

Verdict

The news signal did not hold up out-of-sample. Success was defined before the test was run, and all three criteria had to hold:

  • ✔ Out-of-sample Sharpe ratio above 0 after costs
  • ✔ Beats the same rule without news, out-of-sample
  • ✘ Placebo p-value below 0.05 out-of-sample (real news beats shuffled news)

Settings and audit trail

Signal settings: sentiment_model = finbert, return_days = 5, zscore_window = 60, min_history = 20, threshold = 1.5, holding_days = 5

Every run on the test period is recorded:

  • 2026-10-12T14:13:08+00:00: first run

Limitations

  • Survivorship bias: only companies that still exist today were tested, which flatters results.
  • Headline wording is the latest version; stories edited after publication could look more informed than they were.
  • Trades are assumed to fill at the opening price; real fills can be worse.
  • Research project, not financial advice.
